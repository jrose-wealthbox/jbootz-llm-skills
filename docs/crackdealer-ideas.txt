## Overview

My Apple Music library is ~40K songs. Exported XML is ~88MB. "Date Added" spans ~15 years.

I've put a lot of effort into rating my tracks and creating playlists.

I'd like to use an LLM (such as yourself!) to help me find new music, based on music I already enjoy.

## "New Releases" vs. "New-To-Me" Music

When I say "new" or "new music", assume I mean "music that is new to me."

If I am specifically looking for new releases, such as music released in the last N months, I'll specifically say "new releases" or similar.

## Hunting For New Music

Recommendations for should NOT include tracks I'm already familiar with.

HOWEVER, with a 40K track library, it's possible there are unlistened-to tracks in my own library that I don't even know I'd love. Signals include:

- Play count of 0 is obviously a very strong signal I have never listened to the track
- Play count of 1 or 2 is a somewhat strong signal, especially if the last played date is > 2 years in past

## Playlist Creation / Completion

When I request playlist creation around a musical theme, your recommendations can include a mix of tracks already in my library and tracks not in my library.

Songs I've rated highly are strong candidates for inclusion, although this is not an absolute signal. There may be a lot of great tracks in my library I haven't rated yet.

Some of my playlists are based around a musical theme, genre, or subgenre (ex: "spooky folk psyche", "speed metal")

Other playlists are more like a traditional mixtape or movie soundtrack - songs that are not necessarily from the same genre, but flow together.

If it's not clear from the context of my request, ask me which kind of playlist I'm looking for.

## Deep Cuts / Hidden Gems

"Deep Cuts" and "Hidden Gems" (and similar) terms refer to songs by obscure artists that have few but fervent fans. Or obscure songs by popular artists, such as b-sides or soundtrack songs that get overlooked.

## Initial Architecture Idea

I think this should be an LLM skill that does two things:

- Knows how to convert my 88MB Apple Music exported library .XML into a format like SQLite that can be queried efficiently both in terms of tokens and computation.
- Knows how to query this generated SQLlite file

Beyond that, I would like to simply converse with the LLM "normally" -- I find that LLMs are already good at music recommendations. I basically just want to give them fast efficient access to my existing music library to "turbocharge" their recommendations.

I am not "married" to this architecture whatsoever. Please suggest alternatives and compare/contrast their pros and cons with this initial architecture idea.

IDEA: Perhaps there should be two separate skills: "Skill A"" that knows how to convert/query the Apple Music library, and "Skill B" that uses "Skill A". The possible utility here is that I might build other things that use "Skill A"
